## How to re-check a claim

1. Find the claim in the document and follow its link.
2. Open the primary source, not a summary of it. For a rule, the source is the
   law, the ministry or the standard, not an article about them.
3. Note the date of the source. A rule that changed after the source was
   written is a claim that has expired.
4. If a source cannot be reached or does not support the claim, mark the claim
   `UNVERIFIED` in the document rather than deleting the uncertainty.

## Sources that change

Job platforms change formats, incentives expire, training refresh cycles are
revised. The documents most exposed to drift are the Italian channel and
incentive rules, the European conventions, and every certificate refresh
interval. Re-check those before relying on them.
